Three Ways In

Where Your Music Can Land

01 · The Free Loop

24/7 rotation

The Loop runs music videos around the clock. Accepted videos go into regular rotation alongside everything else on the network — and stay in the on-demand library.

  • Video required — this is a visual rotation
  • Runs across all dayparts
  • You keep every right to your record

Records get spun and talked about during live shows. Different from rotation — here a host actually reacts to it on air, in front of the chat.

  • Often paired with a shout or an interview
  • Clip lives in the episode archive after
03 · Live Music Review

The flagship. Your record goes in front of the panel and gets an honest on-air breakdown — the good and the bad, live, unedited.

  • Bring thick skin — the reviews are real

Before You Send

How to Not Get Skipped

Do

What gets through

  • Send video first. A performance, a music video, a live set or a visualizer. Video travels further on this network than audio.
  • One public link that plays. YouTube is perfectly fine. Unlisted is fine if it isn’t out yet.
  • Clear the rights before you send. Uncleared samples or beats you don’t control can’t be aired.
  • Say what you want — rotation, a review, an interview, all three.
Don’t

What gets skipped

  • Logins and downloads. If we have to make an account or pull a 400MB file, it doesn’t get watched.
  • Expiring transfers. WeTransfer links die before we get to them.
  • Ten links at once. Send your best one.
  • Nothing but a link. Tell us who you are and what we’re listening to.
Submitting is free, and it always will be. We don’t charge for consideration and we don’t run pay-to-play. What that also means: there’s no set turnaround and no guarantee a piece airs. We work through the queue as shows tape. If it’s a fit, we’ll reach out about when it’s going up.
