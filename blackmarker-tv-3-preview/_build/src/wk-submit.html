Three Ways In

Where Your Music Can Land

01 · The Free Loop

24/7 rotation

The Loop runs music videos around the clock. Accepted videos go into regular rotation alongside everything else on the network.

  • Video required — this is a visual rotation
  • Airplay on the Loop — it’s a live rotation, not a VOD library
  • Runs across all dayparts
  • You keep every right to your record
02 · Live Broadcasts

Played on air

Records get spun and talked about during live shows. Different from rotation — here a host actually reacts to it on air, in front of the chat.

  • Audio or video both work
  • Often paired with a shout or an interview
  • Clip lives in the episode archive after
03 · Live Music Review

Can You Dig It!?

The flagship. Your record goes in front of the panel and gets an honest on-air breakdown — the good and the bad, live, unedited.

  • Bring thick skin — the reviews are real
  • Video gets you further than audio alone
  • Full episode stays on demand permanently
Before You Send

How to Not Get Skipped

None of this is us being precious. Every one of these is a real reason something didn’t get watched — and we’d rather you knew before you sent it than wonder afterwards.

Do this
Not this
What you send
✓Video — a performance, music video, live set or visualizer
✕Audio only, when a video of it exists
The link
✓One public link that plays on click. YouTube is perfect
✕Anything behind a login, or a file we have to download
Not out yet?
✓Unlisted YouTube is completely fine
✕Expiring transfers — WeTransfer dies before we reach it
How much
✓Your single best piece
✕Ten links at once and a “pick whichever”
What you tell us
✓Who you are, what we’re hearing, and what you want from it
✕A bare link with no message attached
Rights
✓Samples and beats cleared before you send it
✕Anything uncleared — we legally can’t air it
Submitting is free, and it always will be. We don’t charge for consideration and we don’t run pay-to-play. What that also means: there’s no set turnaround and no guarantee a piece airs. We work through the queue as shows tape. If it’s a fit, we’ll reach out about when it’s going up.
