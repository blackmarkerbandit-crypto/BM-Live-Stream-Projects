Where You Can Run

Four Places to Advertise

01 · The Free Loop

24/7 rotation spots

The Loop runs around the clock — music videos, interviews, performances and station breaks. Your spot drops into that rotation like any other piece of programming.

  • 15–60 second spots in regular rotation
  • We can cut the spot for you if you don’t have one
  • Runs across every daypart, or targeted to specific blocks
02 · Show Sponsorship

Own a show

Attach your brand to a single show — Can You Dig It!?, The Weekly Interrupt, For The Record, The Alien Podcast. Host reads land differently than a banner.

  • Live host read plus on-screen lower third
  • Presence in the permanent on-demand episode, not just the live airing
  • Show-page and social credit
03 · Live Event Broadcasts

Battles, festivals, parades

Our event streams are the highest-energy thing we do. Sponsor the broadcast itself — on-air, on-banner, and in the archive afterwards.

  • Naming and on-air presence for the full broadcast
  • Pre-event promo run-up across the Loop and the site
  • Play data with viewer geography after the event
04 · Event Promotion

Promote your event to our audience

Packages that push your event across the site, the Loop and our socials. We’re selective here — we only take events we’d actually tell people about.

  • Site placement plus Loop promo spots
  • Social posts across YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Twitch
  • Optional: we broadcast the event too (see Production Services)
