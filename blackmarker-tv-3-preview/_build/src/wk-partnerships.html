Four Kinds of Partner

01 · Content Partners

Shows, creators and catalogues

Bring a show to the network or license your catalogue into the rotation. You keep making it; we handle broadcast, distribution and the on-demand archive.

  • A slot on the 24/7 Free Loop plus a permanent on-demand home
  • Studio time and crew if you need production, not just placement
  • Cross-promotion across the network’s shows and socials
  • You keep your IP — we’re a distribution partner, not an owner
02 · Event Sponsors

Put your name on a broadcast

Back a battle card, a festival stream, a parade or a conference. Sponsorship covers the broadcast itself, not just a logo on a flyer.

  • Naming and on-air presence across the live broadcast
  • Branding baked into the permanent archive, not just the live window
  • Promo run-up across the Loop and the site ahead of the event
  • Post-event play data from ChannelCast, including viewer geography
03 · Production Partners

Crews, venues and vendors

For venues, promoters and production companies who need a broadcast arm — or who have capacity we can call on when a job runs bigger than one crew.

  • White-label broadcast delivery under your brand
  • Referral and subcontract work in both directions
  • Shared kit and crew on events neither side could staff alone
  • A streaming platform to hand clients after the show — not just a file
04 · Investment

Capital and strategic backing

We’re a profitable production business building a network on top of it. If that shape interests you, the numbers conversation is open.

  • Existing production revenue funds the network build
  • ChannelCast — the white-label streaming platform — is the scalable layer
  • Clear use of funds: platform, programming, and audience acquisition
  • Serious enquiries get real financials under NDA
