Your Material

Video is what we push hardest — a performance, a music video, a live set, a visualizer. A plain YouTube link is perfectly fine. Audio-only is welcome too, but video gets more airtime and travels further on the network. Submitting is free — we work through the queue as shows tape, so there’s no set turnaround.

What makes a good submission

  • Send video first. Performance, music video, live set, or visualizer.
  • One link, publicly playable. No logins, no downloads, no expiring file transfers.
  • Unlisted YouTube is fine if it isn’t out yet — just make sure the link plays.
  • Say what you want. A review, a feature, an interview, rotation — tell us.
  • Clear the rights first. Uncleared samples or beats you don’t control can’t be aired.
