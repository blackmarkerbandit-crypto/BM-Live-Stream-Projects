Rooms

One chat per room. Nothing crosses between them — messages, moderators and bans are all room-scoped. public is listed and open. unlisted is not listed anywhere, but anyone who learns the slug can read and post, so it relies on the page around it being gated and the slug not being guessable — right for a PPV room the CMS protects. restricted admits only people added to room_members, which needs something server-side to add them; until that exists a restricted room is readable by nobody but its moderators. Archiving keeps a room readable but stops new messages.

Moderators

A moderator only sees and acts on the rooms listed here. Room * means every room, now and in future. Moderators must sign in with a real email account — an anonymous identity lives in browser storage and is lost the moment cookies are cleared.
